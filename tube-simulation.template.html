PVC pipe: magnet sliding through coils

Coupled screening model · regular waves · pipe heaves with the surface · magnet is a base-excited mass · load resistance auto-matched
Suspension, winding and electrical assumptions
A sealed pipe floats and follows the wave. Inside, an axially magnetised magnet slides in the bore; coils wound outside see its flux move and the induced current opposes the motion. Repelling end magnets are scaled to hold the magnet's own weight at the levitation gap: with no added spring they are the suspension, otherwise they are end bumpers and the magnet is preloaded to mid-travel. Coil inductance is neglected (L/R is milliseconds against a multi-second wave) and flagged when that fails. Load resistance 0 = search for the power-maximising value. See MODEL.md for what is not modeled.
