Full product: wave → float → water column → jet → piezo leaf

Coupled screening model · regular waves · RK4 time integration · reports the last three settled wave cycles
Hull, damping, coupling, leaf, electrical & charging assumptions
Float heaves on the wave; a vertical water tube under it pumps water through a nozzle onto the tip of a clamped PZT-5A/steel leaf (the strip model). One-way ideal routing; see MODEL.md for what is not modeled.
Sources: model.mjs, full-model.mjs (checked by node check.mjs, node check-full.mjs). Rebuild this page with node build.mjs.
