    What the numbers mean

    This is a two-body heave and load-screening model with assumed hardware coefficients. It does not establish survivability, station-keeping capability, fatigue life, or dock capture success. Forces are signed along the positive travel axis. Wind/gust and underwater drag are calculated separately; gust is an envelope, not a measured time history.

    Model, sources and limitations

    NOAA NDBC station 46005, 2024: simultaneous valid wave-height, dominant-period and mean-wind observations. Presets select actual timestamped rows by wave-height percentile; wind is taken from that same row. Current is user-assumed. This is one ocean location, not worldwide coverage.

    Irregular waves use 24 deterministic-phase Pierson–Moskowitz components normalized to Hs/4 variance. DPD is used as the peak period, not the energy period. Linear deep-water decay exp(−kL) changes excitation at depth. Coupled heave includes connection stiffness EA/L, PTO damping, added mass, restoring stiffness and equivalent viscous damping. Horizontal connection drag is integrated over 12 depth segments.

    Fixed assumptions: effective masses 11 / 18 tonnes; waterplane 12 m²; lower restoring 2 kN/m; radiation damping 7 / 5 kN·s/m; effective connection EA 100 kN; 2 kW electrical ceiling; 68% efficiency; 18% mechanical allocation to foils with 45% assumed propulsive efficiency. Length is the mean submerged depth of a vertical compliant guide/tether arrangement, not a designed solid structural pillar.

    Two-way PTO damping assumes an engineered bidirectional mechanism. Support paths must permit PTO travel; taut fixed-length parallel tethers would bypass it. Battery mass is lumped into the lower effective mass. Pressure pods, structural stiffness, drag and foil efficiency are not validated from the illustrative CAD. No slamming, breaking-wave impact, mooring, pitch/roll, tether bending, buckling, seabed clearance or full hydrodynamic interaction is resolved.

    Storms above Hs 4.5 m switch off generation and charging. Motion/load outputs then remain unvalidated linear extrapolations. Exceeding ±1.5 m stroke or 40 kN dynamic connection force flags an invalid operating envelope; neither threshold is a certified hardware rating. Model power is not guaranteed deliverable power outside that envelope.
